# 🚀 Getting Started Guide
## Mammogram Viewer & Annotation System

---

## Welcome!

This guide will help you get started with the Mammogram Viewer & Annotation System. Follow these simple steps to begin uploading and managing your medical images.

---

## 📋 What You'll Need

Before you start, make sure you have:

✅ **License Key** - Provided by your system administrator  
✅ **Email Address** - For your account  
✅ **Strong Password** - At least 8 characters  
✅ **Internet Connection** - Stable connection required  

---

## Step 1: Registration

### 1.1 Access the Registration Page

1. Open your web browser
2. Navigate to the application URL (provided by your admin)
3. Click on **"Register"** or **"Sign Up"** button

### 1.2 Fill in Your Information

Enter the following details:

```
✓ Full Name: Your complete name
✓ Email Address: Your work email
✓ Password: Create a strong password (min 8 characters)
✓ Confirm Password: Re-enter your password
✓ License Key: Enter the key provided by your admin
  Format: AMB-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX
```

### 1.3 Submit Registration

1. Review all information carefully
2. Click **"Register"** button
3. You'll see a confirmation message
4. Wait for admin approval (usually within 24 hours)

### 1.4 Check Your Email

- You'll receive an email when your account is approved
- The email contains your login instructions
- Keep this email for reference

---

## Step 2: First Login

### 2.1 Access the Login Page

1. Go to the application URL
2. Click **"Login"** button

### 2.2 Enter Your Credentials

```
✓ Email: Your registered email address
✓ Password: The password you created
```

### 2.3 Login

1. Click **"Login"** button
2. You'll be redirected to your dashboard
3. Welcome screen appears (first time only)

---

## Step 3: Explore Your Dashboard

### 3.1 Dashboard Overview

Your dashboard shows:

- **License Status**: Your license validity and expiration
- **Upload Quota**: How many images you can upload
- **Recent Images**: Your recently uploaded images
- **Quick Actions**: Buttons for common tasks

### 3.2 Main Navigation

The main menu includes:

- **Dashboard**: Home page with overview
- **Upload Images**: Upload new medical images
- **My Images**: View all your uploaded images
- **Profile**: Manage your account settings

---

## Step 4: Check Your License Status

### 4.1 View License Information

On your dashboard, you'll see:

```
License Status: Active ✓
Expires: [Date]
Days Remaining: [Number]

Upload Quota:
Used: [Number] / [Total]
Remaining: [Number] images
```

### 4.2 Understanding Status Indicators

- **🟢 Active**: Your license is valid, you can upload
- **🟡 Expiring Soon**: Less than 30 days remaining
- **🔴 Expired**: License expired, contact admin
- **⚫ Revoked**: License terminated, contact admin

### 4.3 Quota Warnings

You'll see warnings when:
- **80% used**: Yellow warning - plan ahead
- **90% used**: Orange alert - contact admin soon
- **100% used**: Red alert - cannot upload, contact admin

---

## Step 5: Your First Upload

### 5.1 Navigate to Upload

1. Click **"Upload Images"** from dashboard
2. Or click **"Upload"** in the main menu

### 5.2 Select Your Files

1. Click **"Choose Files"** button
2. Or drag and drop files into the upload area
3. Select one or more image files

**Supported Formats:**
- DICOM files (.dcm, .dicom)
- JPEG images (.jpg, .jpeg)
- PNG images (.png)
- TIFF images (.tiff, .tif)
- AAN files (.aan)
- ZIP archives (.zip)

### 5.3 Fill in Patient Information

Enter required details:

```
Required Fields:
✓ Patient ID: Unique identifier
✓ Patient Name: Full name
✓ Study Date: Date of examination
✓ Modality: Type (e.g., Mammography)

Optional Fields:
○ Patient Age: Age in years
○ Patient Sex: M/F/O
○ Study Description: Brief description
○ Institution Name: Your facility name
```

### 5.4 Upload

1. Review all information
2. Click **"Upload"** button
3. Wait for upload to complete
4. Success message appears
5. Images appear in your gallery

---

## Step 6: View Your Images

### 6.1 Access Image Gallery

1. Click **"My Images"** from dashboard
2. All your uploaded images appear as thumbnails

### 6.2 Image Information

Each image shows:
- Thumbnail preview
- Patient ID
- Study date
- Upload date
- File format

### 6.3 Search and Filter

Use the search bar to find images by:
- Patient ID
- Patient name
- Date range
- Modality

---

## Step 7: Basic Image Viewing

### 7.1 Open an Image

1. Click on any image thumbnail
2. Image viewer opens in full screen

### 7.2 Viewer Controls

**Zoom:**
- Mouse wheel up: Zoom in
- Mouse wheel down: Zoom out
- **+** button: Zoom in
- **-** button: Zoom out

**Pan:**
- Click and drag to move image

**Reset:**
- Click **"Fit to Screen"** to reset view

### 7.3 Close Viewer

- Click **"X"** button
- Or press **Esc** key

---

## Step 8: Update Your Profile

### 8.1 Access Profile

1. Click on your name/icon (top right)
2. Select **"Profile"** or **"Settings"**

### 8.2 View Your Information

You can see:
- Your name and email
- Account status
- License information
- Registration date

### 8.3 Change Password

1. Go to **"Change Password"** section
2. Enter current password
3. Enter new password
4. Confirm new password
5. Click **"Update Password"**
