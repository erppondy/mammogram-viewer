## 🎮 How to Use

### Drawing a Polygon:
1. Press **P** or click "Polygon" tool
2. Click on image to add points
3. **Double-click** to close polygon
4. Press **Escape** to cancel

### Drawing Freehand:
1. Press **F** or click "Freehand" tool
2. Click and drag on image
3. Release to finish
4. Annotation is automatically saved

### Using Undo/Redo:
1. Make changes to annotations
2. Press **Ctrl+Z** to undo
3. Press **Ctrl+Y** to redo
4. Full history preserved

### Selecting Annotations:
1. Click on annotation in the list
2. Annotation highlights in green on image
3. Press **Delete** to remove
4. Click elsewhere to deselect

### Adjusting Image:
1. Use **Brightness** slider for visibility
2. Use **Contrast** slider for clarity
3. Adjust in real-time
4. Perfect for medical images
